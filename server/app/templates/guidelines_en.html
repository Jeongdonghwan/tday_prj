Community Guidelines

So everyone can share their love stories safely

1. Respect each other

No harassment, slurs, hate speech, or discriminatory remarks toward any person or group. Disagree with kindness.

2. Prohibited content

  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content
  • Anything inappropriate involving minors
  • Targeting or doxxing specific individuals (exposing personal information)
  • Content that promotes violence, self-harm, or illegal activity
  • Spam, ads, flooding, scams, or phishing

3. Protect privacy

Do not post real names, contact info, addresses, workplaces, or any details that could identify yourself or others.

4. Report and block

You can report inappropriate posts and comments, and block users so their posts and comments no longer appear to you. Content that accumulates reports is automatically hidden and reviewed by our team.

5. Enforcement

Content that violates these guidelines may be hidden without prior notice. Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted access or account suspension.
